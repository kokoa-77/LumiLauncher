# Code of Conduct · 行为准则

## English

### Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

### Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Spam, advertising, and sharing cracked game builds, account trading or malware
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

### Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all of Lumi's official spaces: this GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, discussions), the official Discord server, the official QQ group and QQ Channel, and any other channel listed in [TRADEMARK.md](TRADEMARK.md). It also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

### Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, discussions, and messages that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and will explain moderation decisions when appropriate.

### Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, send a private message to the maintainer (`kokoa-77`) on Discord or in the QQ group, or to an administrator of that space. All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the reporter will be respected.

### Enforcement

Depending on the severity, maintainers may respond with:

1. **A reminder**: a private, written note explaining what was inappropriate.
2. **A warning**: a warning with consequences for continued behavior, and a period without interacting with the people involved.
3. **A temporary ban**: a temporary ban from interaction or public communication in the community.
4. **A permanent ban**: for repeated violations, harassment, or sharing malware or other content that puts players at risk.

## 简体中文

> 本译文仅供参考；如有出入，以英文版为准。

### 我们的承诺

身为社区成员、贡献者和维护者，我们承诺：无论年龄、体型、显性或隐性的身心障碍、民族、生理性别特征、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育程度、社会经济地位、国籍、外貌、种族、种姓、肤色、宗教信仰、性取向如何，每个人都能在参与本社区时免受骚扰。

### 我们的准则

有助于营造良好社区氛围的行为包括：

- 对他人表现出同理心和善意
- 尊重不同的意见、观点和经历
- 提出并虚心接受建设性的反馈
- 为自己的过错承担责任，向受影响的人道歉，并从中吸取教训
- 不只考虑个人得失，而是着眼于整个社区的利益

不可接受的行为包括：

- 使用带有性暗示的言语或图像，以及任何形式的性关注或性骚扰
- 恶意挑衅、侮辱或贬损性的评论，以及人身攻击或政治攻击
- 公开或私下的骚扰
- 未经明确许可，公开他人的隐私信息，例如住址或邮箱地址
- 刷屏、打广告，以及分享破解版游戏、账号交易或恶意程序
- 其他在正式场合中会被合理认为不恰当的行为

### 适用范围

本准则适用于 Lumi 的所有官方场所：本 GitHub 仓库（Issue、PR、Discussions）、官方 Discord 服务器、官方 QQ 群与 QQ 频道，以及 [TRADEMARK.md](TRADEMARK.md#简体中文) 中列出的其他渠道；当有人在公共场合正式代表本社区时也同样适用。

### 执行职责

项目维护者负责解释并执行本准则，对任何不恰当、带有威胁、冒犯或伤害性的行为采取适当且公正的处理措施。

维护者有权删除、编辑或拒绝不符合本准则的评论、提交、代码、Issue、讨论和消息，并会在适当的时候说明处理理由。

### 举报

如果你遭遇或目睹了不可接受的行为，请私信维护者（`kokoa-77`，Discord 或 QQ 群均可），或联系该场所的管理员。所有举报都会被及时、公正地处理，并保护举报人的隐私。

### 处理方式

维护者会视情节轻重采取以下措施：

1. **提醒**：私下书面说明哪里不妥。
2. **警告**：说明继续违反的后果，并在一段时间内不得与相关人员互动。
3. **临时封禁**：在一段时间内禁止在社区中互动或公开发言。
4. **永久封禁**：针对屡次违规、骚扰，或传播恶意程序等危及玩家安全的行为。
